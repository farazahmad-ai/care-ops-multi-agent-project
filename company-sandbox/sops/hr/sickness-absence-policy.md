# CareOps Ltd: Sickness & Absence Policy

## 1. Notification
Carers unfit to attend a scheduled shift must notify the Operations on-call desk by telephone at least **4 hours prior** to shift commencement. Text messages and emails are not accepted.

## 2. Evidence of Incapacity
Absences lasting up to 7 consecutive days require a self-certification form. Absences exceeding 7 calendar days require a Statement of Fitness for Work (Fit Note) issued by a registered healthcare professional (`TODO: verify against current regulator guidance`).
