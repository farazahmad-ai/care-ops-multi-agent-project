# CareOps Ltd: Annual Leave Policy (Current v2.1)

## 1. Overview
This policy governs statutory and contractual annual leave entitlements for all employed care assistants and office staff at CareOps Ltd. 

> *Note: Statutory figures and legal frameworks must be checked against current UK employment legislation (`TODO: verify against current regulator guidance`).*

## 2. Requesting Leave
All carers must submit annual leave requests via the CareOps management portal at least **4 weeks (28 calendar days)** in advance of the first requested day of absence. 
- Leave requests submitted with less than 28 days' notice will be rejected automatically unless exceptional compassionate grounds exist.
- Overlapping leave requests will not be approved.
