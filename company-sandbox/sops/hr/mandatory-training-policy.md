# CareOps Ltd: Mandatory Training Policy

All active care staff must maintain up-to-date certifications in core competencies:
- Moving & Handling (Refresher required annually)
- Safeguarding Vulnerable Adults (Level 2 refresher required annually)
- Basic Life Support & First Aid (Annual practical verification)

Note that DBS checks should also be renewed periodically, typically every 3 years or when requested by local authority safeguarding partners.
