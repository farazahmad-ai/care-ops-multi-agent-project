# CareOps Ltd: Onboarding Procedure

Prior to placing any newly recruited carer onto active rotas, the HR team must confirm:
1. An Enhanced DBS certificate issued within the past 12 months with barred list checks.
2. Verified Right-to-Work documentation under Home Office regulations (`TODO: verify against current regulator guidance`).
3. Satisfactory completion of the Care Certificate induction modules.
4. Two verified professional references from previous healthcare or social care employers.
