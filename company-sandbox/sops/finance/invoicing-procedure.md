# CareOps Ltd: Invoicing Procedure

1. Client invoices are generated monthly in arrears covering the first to the final calendar day of each month.
2. Invoices only aggregate approved, non-disputed timesheets.
3. Rates are applied according to the contracted schedule of rates (Standard Carer: £21.00/hr, Senior Carer: £25.50/hr).
4. Credit terms are strictly 30 days net from invoice date.
