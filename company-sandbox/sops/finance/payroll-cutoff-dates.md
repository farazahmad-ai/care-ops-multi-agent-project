# CareOps Ltd: Payroll Schedule

1. The weekly payroll cut-off is strictly Monday at 12:00 PM for all shifts worked up to Sunday midnight.
2. Any timesheet submitted or approved after Monday midday rolls over into the subsequent pay cycle.
3. Carer bank transfers are executed every Thursday via BACS.
