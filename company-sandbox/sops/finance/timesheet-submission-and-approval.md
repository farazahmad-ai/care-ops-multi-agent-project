# CareOps Ltd: Timesheet Approval

## 1. Submission
Carers must submit electronic timesheets via the portal within 24 hours of completing a shift.
- Claimed hours cannot exceed shift duration plus a 1-hour handover grace period.
- Negative or zero hours are rejected.

## 2. Approvals
Timesheets are approved by Finance or Operations supervisors. 
*Note on Senior Carers:* Standard carers require explicit office verification. For Senior Carers acting as team leads on duty, procedures state both that timesheets require Registered Manager counter-signature and that lead staff may self-authorize time logs for direct client sign-off.
