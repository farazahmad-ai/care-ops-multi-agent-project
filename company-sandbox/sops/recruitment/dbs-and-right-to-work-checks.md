# CareOps Ltd: DBS & Right to Work Compliance

## 1. Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS)
All staff engaged in regulated healthcare activities must hold a valid Enhanced DBS Certificate registered with the DBS Update Service.
- CareOps company policy strictly mandates an **annual (every 12 months)** electronic re-check of the DBS status for all active carers.
- If a DBS check expires, the carer is immediately suspended from all rosters.
