# CareOps Ltd: Interview Process

1. All candidates progressing past initial screening must attend a competency-based interview conducted by a Recruitment Officer or Registered Branch Manager.
2. The interview evaluates values-based care, dementia awareness, safeguarding scenarios, and communication skills.
3. Post-interview outcomes must be recorded within 24 hours as `pass`, `fail`, or `no_show`.
