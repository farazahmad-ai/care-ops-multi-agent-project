# CareOps Ltd: Offer and Hiring Approval

## 1. Approval Authority
Formal written offers of employment can only be authorized by a Recruitment Lead or HR Director.

## 2. Hiring Prerequisites
Before moving a candidate from `offered` to `hired`:
1. Both DBS and Right-to-Work verification records must be registered in the system.
2. The recruitment officer must complete paper Form HR-7B (Candidate Final Clearance Sheet) and enter the physical voucher ID into CarePro Legacy v3.
