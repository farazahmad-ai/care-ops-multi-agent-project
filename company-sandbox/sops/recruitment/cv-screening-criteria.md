# CareOps Ltd: Candidate CV Screening

When evaluating initial candidate applications:
1. Minimum of 6 months previous experience in domiciliary or residential care (preferred, not mandatory for trainee roles).
2. Continuous employment history with all gaps exceeding 30 calendar days documented and explained.
3. Residence within 15 miles or a 45-minute commute of client care homes.
4. Valid UK contact telephone and email. Applications missing contact information must be placed in `rejected` status.
