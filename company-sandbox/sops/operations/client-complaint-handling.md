# CareOps Ltd: Client Complaints

1. All verbal and written complaints from care homes regarding staff performance, lateness, or attitude must be logged in the operations incident log within 2 hours of receipt.
2. An investigation must commence within 24 hours, coordinated by the Registered Care Manager.
3. Carers under active investigation for safeguarding concerns must be immediately set to `suspended` status.
