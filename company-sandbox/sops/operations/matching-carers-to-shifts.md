# CareOps Ltd: Matching Carers to Shifts

Operational controllers matching carers to shifts must observe four mandatory checks:
1. Compliance validity (DBS and Right to Work valid on shift date).
2. Absence verification (carer must not be on approved annual or sick leave).
3. Schedule overlap (no concurrent or conflicting shift times).
4. Competency/role tier (a Senior Carer can cover standard carer shifts, but standard carers cannot cover senior carer shifts).
