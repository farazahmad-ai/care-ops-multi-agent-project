# CareOps Ltd: Last-Minute Cover Procedure

## 1. Urgent Cover Trigger
An unfilled shift is officially classified as an **Urgent Cover Requirement** whenever shift commencement is **less than 24 hours away**.

## 2. Dispatch Workflow
1. Query available carers within an 8-mile geographic radius.
2. Confirm compliance status (DBS, Right-to-Work valid on shift date).
3. Confirm carer is not currently on approved leave and has no overlapping shift assignment.
4. Senior care shifts can only be filled by accredited Senior Carers.
