# CareOps Ltd: Shift Cancellation Rules

## 1. Client Notice Periods
Clients cancelling scheduled shifts must provide at least 24 hours' notice. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours notice will incur the standard 4-hour minimum charge.

## 2. Emergency Operational Status
Operations controllers must initiate urgent standby rosters for any vacancy arising **with less than 48 hours notice**, notifying regional team leaders immediately.
