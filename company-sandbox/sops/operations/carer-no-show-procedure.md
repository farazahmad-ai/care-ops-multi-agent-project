# CareOps Ltd: Carer No-Show Procedure

1. If a carer fails to arrive within 15 minutes of shift start, the operations controller must attempt contact via telephone immediately.
2. If unreachable within 30 minutes, update the shift assignment status to `no_show`.
3. Initiate the Last-Minute Cover Procedure immediately to prevent client service failure.
